How confidently can I read this result?

Aircraft movement uses published and literature-supported inputs. Gate geometry, preparation behavior and frustration coefficients remain provisional pending validation.

“Accumulated during preparation” and “accumulated during embarkation” attribute burden to time periods. They do not prove that either phase caused the feeling.

Method & sources

What this adds to the boarding-only clock

The three-way presentation is inspired by Adam Jacobs’s visualization of Random, Back-to-front and Strict Steffen boarding. That work starts the visible race at boarding; Boarding Lab extends the clock backward to the first preparation instruction.

This standalone research simulator is deterministic for a given seed. Its source notes and validation boundaries distinguish published aircraft mechanics from provisional preparation and frustration inputs.
